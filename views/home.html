04 / HOW IT WORKS

How the maps are made

Source code, equations, data lineage and limitations are public because transparency matters more than a convincing-looking plume.

01

Collect forecast grids

Background jobs check for newly available model runs and request winds from Open-Meteo's NOAA GFS / RRFS and ECMWF APIs. Source fields include wind speed and meteorological direction at 10 m, 80 m, 925 hPa and 850 hPa. Each model has its own download time. If a refresh fails, the site keeps the last validated data for the same scenario date.

02

Interpolate weather

The site converts wind speed/direction to east/north velocity, then interpolates those vectors between surrounding geographic sample points and adjacent forecast hours. API point forecasts are samples, not a native, perfectly resolved model grid.

03

Integrate parcel paths

An idealized parcel starts at the plant at the selected release time and stays on one nominal wind level. Midpoint (second-order Runge–Kutta / RK2) integration advances it in 15-minute increments, until eight hours or until winds are unavailable.

WIND COMPONENTS
u = −S sin(θ)   ·   v = −S cos(θ)

Where θ is the direction the wind comes from, measured clockwise from north, and S is its speed.

TRAJECTORY INTEGRATION
xn+1 = xn + Δt × V(xmid, tmid)

A 15-minute midpoint step, using winds sampled at the projected midpoint.

What this model cannot tell you

It does not model explosion physics, release mass, particle-size distributions, height of the actual cloud, dry/wet deposition, resuspension, atmospheric turbulence, vertical mixing or dose. The shaded band compares near-surface wind paths from different models at the same forecast times. It does not show plume width, probability or lead exposure. It is not NOAA HYSPLIT or an EPA regulatory dispersion model, and it has not been calibrated against observations.

Crucially: the presence or absence of a wind trajectory cannot prove contamination or safety. Consult official modeling and measured air and soil results for exposure information.
